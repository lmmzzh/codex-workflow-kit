# Handoff Retrospective

## Purpose

Turn this round's result into a concise handoff that another session can resume from.

## Workflow

1. Check what actually changed, what remained untouched, and what was or was not verified.
2. Decide whether this round needs a handoff, a retrospective, or both.
3. Fill the corresponding template in `assets/`.
4. Make touched files, untouched boundaries, risk scope, and next entry points explicit.
5. If implementation was not locally verified, state that clearly and do not imply completion.

## Do Not

- Do not hide unverified items.
- Do not claim untouched work as completed.
- Do not write a long background essay.

## Output

Produce a practical handoff and optionally a retrospective if the round was large or multi-step.
Prefer concrete file/doc references, open risks, and next-step instructions over broad summaries.
